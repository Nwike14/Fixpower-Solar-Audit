This tool applies standard off-grid/hybrid solar sizing arithmetic. It doesn't pull live data or pricing — every figure comes only from what's entered above, so a FixPower engineer will confirm the final specification before installation.

Array size

kWp = (daily kWh use × design margin) ÷ peak sun hours

Battery bank

usable kWh = daily kWh × autonomy days bank kWh = usable kWh ÷ depth of discharge bank Ah = (bank kWh × 1000) ÷ bus voltage

Inverter

inverter design peak = connected load × max(diversity factor, 0.8) inverter kVA = inverter design peak × surge allowance × future growth margin, rounded up to nearest standard size

The battery and energy figures use your actual diversity factor. The inverter itself uses a minimum of 0.8 even if your diversity factor is lower, plus a future growth margin (default 15%) — a percentage scales sensibly across small and large systems, rather than adding the same flat kVA to every job.
